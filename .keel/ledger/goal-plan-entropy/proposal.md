# Proposal

## Problem / why

Entropy scanning reports the intentional long-horizon goal record as an orphaned change plan.

## Objective

Recognize explicitly marked goal records without weakening orphan detection for ordinary active execution plans.

## Non-goals

Do not suppress untracked plans generally or alter lifecycle ledger requirements.

## Success evidence

Focused tests prove marked goal records are accepted while ordinary orphan plans remain findings.

## Open decisions

The marker is a stable heading at the beginning of the goal record.
