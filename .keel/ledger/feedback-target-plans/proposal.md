# Proposal

## Problem / why

Feedback queue candidates lack a concrete, reproducible target-evaluation or promotion contract.

## Objective

Emit deterministic target plans that preserve evidence inputs and required output boundaries without executing them.

## Non-goals

No target execution, scheduler, automatic promotion, or policy mutation.

## Success evidence

Focused tests prove target plans for reviewed and evaluated observations and preserve read-only behavior.

## Open decisions

Target runner and evaluation corpus remain operator-provided.
