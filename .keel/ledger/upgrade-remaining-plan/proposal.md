# Proposal

## Problem / why

The audit identifies unresolved local and prerequisite-dependent upgrades, but their execution boundaries and acceptance evidence are not yet represented as one durable work plan.

## Objective

Record each remaining upgrade as an executable workstream with owner boundary, prerequisite, acceptance evidence, and deferral rationale.

## Non-goals

Do not claim implementation, create external integrations, or authorize runtime/provider access.

## Success evidence

The plan covers every non-implemented audit row, distinguishes local implementation from external prerequisites, and updates the audit and long-horizon goal state.

## Open decisions

External runtime and tracker choices remain operator decisions.
